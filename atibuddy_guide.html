How to get started
3 Simple Steps
1

Search for AtiBuddy in Google Chat

Open Google Chat, click on the search bar at the top and type "AtiBuddy". You will see AtiBuddy appear under Apps. Click on it and then click "Start chat".

2

Install AtiBuddy

Click "Install app" when prompted. AtiBuddy is built by Atidiv IT and is safe to install. Once installed, it will appear in your Apps section in Google Chat.

3

Type "hi" and get started!

Once installed, open AtiBuddy and type "hi". AtiBuddy will welcome you and show the main menu. Choose Create New Ticket to raise an IT issue or Reopen Existing Ticket to follow up.

TL Tip: As a Team Leader, select "Create New Ticket" then choose "Other User" to raise a ticket on behalf of a team member. Enter their name and Employee ID.
